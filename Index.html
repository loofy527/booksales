Our Story

Founded in 1995 by retired librarian Margaret Greene, Chapter & Verse began as a small corner shop with just 500 books. Today, we proudly house over 20,000 titles across all genres.

We believe in the magic of physical books - the smell of ink on paper, the weight of a book in your hands, and the joy of discovering new worlds between covers.

Our passionate staff are all avid readers who love matching people with their next favorite book. We host weekly book clubs, author signings, and reading events for all ages.
